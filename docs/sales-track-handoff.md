# Sales track handoff

## Product boundary

Sales experiences answer **why this matters and which path fits**. Numbered technical labs answer **how it works and what the learner can prove**.

Sales experiences therefore reuse the certified technical journey through a handoff. They do not duplicate technical runtimes, consume certification seats, or become certified merely because the destination lab is certified.

## Existing entry experiences

| Entry experience | Purpose | Recommended technical handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red Hat × Intel AI Strategy — Choose the Path to Production | Portfolio-level discovery and track selection | Agentic AI, Sovereign AI, Virtualization + AI, or AI on Intel Xeon entry |
| AI on Intel Xeon — Start Where the Workload Fits | Qualify inference fit and CPU-first opportunities | Intel AI 101 or Agentic AI 201 |
| Governed Agentic AI — Earn Trust Before Authority | Frame agentic value, risk, and authority | Agentic AI 101 for foundations; deeper levels only when the audience already has the prerequisites |
| Sovereign AI — Control What You Can Prove | Frame sovereignty, evidence, and control boundaries | Sovereign AI 101 |
| Virtualization + AI — Modernize Without Waiting | Frame VM and AI modernization choices | Virtualization + AI 101 |

All five existing entry experiences are signed and factory `intake_ready`, but they have zero Launchpad-certified seats and are not currently orderable catalog labs.

## Governed Agentic AI routing

The default path should be:

`Governed Agentic AI sales entry → Agentic AI 101 → Agentic AI 201 → Agentic AI 301 → Agentic AI 401 → Agentic AI 501 → Agentic AI 601`

The sales entry may route an experienced audience directly to a deeper level only when the prerequisite and intended outcome are explicit. It must not imply that a seven-minute sales story proves production authority.
