== The situation

Your team has an AI assistant that can explain service health and can propose a restart. The generated answer may look confident, but confidence cannot answer the questions that matter:

. What classified the request?
. Which tool could the workflow use?
. What evidence supported the result?
. Which policy controlled the decision?
. Who retained authority to change the service?
. Could another operator reconstruct the run?

You will trace two requests through one workflow. The first only investigates. The second asks for a state-changing action.

== The operating pattern

[source,text]
----
Ask → Plan → Use → Prove → Decide → Record
----

The model proposes. A bounded tool returns evidence. Deterministic policy controls permission. A person retains consequential authority. The trace records what happened.

== What you will leave with

You will create a small workflow map that identifies request class, tool boundary, evidence source, policy result, authority owner, and mutation state. That map becomes the design input for Agentic AI 201.
